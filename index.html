Research Focus

My research focuses on Soft Robotics, Compliant Mechanisms, and Bio-Inspired Mechanics[cite: 1]. I specialize in bridging computational geometric design, finite element analysis (FEA), dynamic simulation, and multi-material additive manufacturing to realize highly adaptive physical prototypes[cite: 1, 2].
