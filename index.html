Flexipede: bio-inspired modular robot for rough terrain

  • Led mechanical design and parametric CAD of the modular compliant joints and interconnects, at the MAIM Lab, University of Dhaka.
  • Ran non-linear FEA in ANSYS for stress distribution, dynamic deformation and fatigue limits.
  • Managed FDM printing, metrology, BOMs and component sourcing for the physical prototypes.
  • Co-authored the paper in Robotics (MDPI), DOI link. The project won a silver medal at WICE and 4th place at GRIC.
Soft roboticsCompliant flexuresANSYS

GeckoBot: climbing robot with compliant adhesion

  • Research assistant on a project funded by the ICT Division, Government of Bangladesh.
  • Engineered bio-inspired compliant adhesion mechanisms and soft contact interfaces.
  • Built CAD models and ANSYS stress-deformation simulations to optimize adhesion force distribution and flexibility.
  • Fabricated multi-material FDM prototypes and led the experimental validation of climbing performance.
Undergraduate thesisOrigami-inspiredIn-pipe robot

Origami-inspired inchworm robot for in-pipe maintenance

  • Undergraduate thesis (2025 to 2026) on an inchworm robot that moves with minimal actuation.
  • Uses compliant origami structures and proprioceptive terrain awareness.
  • Focus on underactuated dynamic structures, kinematic deformation under geometric constraints and soft sensor integration.
